Job Summary

Oracle is hiring fresh graduates and early-career professionals for the role of Support Engineer 1. You will provide technical support to Oracle customers, troubleshoot product issues, document solutions, collaborate with engineering teams, and deliver exceptional customer service while learning Oracle technologies and cloud solutions.

Educational Qualification

  • Any Bachelor's Degree.
  • Eligible Batch: 2024 / 2025 / 2026.
  • Freshers and candidates with up to 2 years of experience can apply.
  • Good English communication skills are required.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide technical support to Oracle customers via phone and electronic channels.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve customer technical issues.
  • Research, recreate, test, and validate reported product issues.
  • Document service requests and maintain technical records.
  • Create and update troubleshooting guides and Knowledge Base articles.
  • Collaborate with senior engineers and internal support teams.
  • Monitor support performance metrics and ensure timely issue resolution.
  • Suggest improvements to support processes and workflows.

Required Skills

  • Oracle Database & SQL Basics
  • Java or Python Fundamentals
  • Linux / Unix Basics
  • Troubleshooting & Debugging Skills
  • Problem Solving & Analytical Thinking
  • Customer Service Mindset
  • Team Collaboration
  • Technical Documentation
  • Good Verbal & Written Communication

Preferred Skills

  • Basic understanding of Cloud Computing.
  • Knowledge of Oracle Products.
  • Experience with AI-assisted troubleshooting tools.
  • Knowledge Base documentation experience.
  • Cross-functional collaboration skills.
